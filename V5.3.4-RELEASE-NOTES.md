# FEDUSA Congress Management Platform v5.3.4

## Voting-session check-in update

- Congress check-in and voting-session check-in remain separate scanner modes.
- All delegates can complete congress check-in.
- Voting-session scanning verifies the individual delegate voting field, regardless of whether the delegate is an affiliate attendee or an NOB / International Delegate.
- Affiliate attendees marked **Primary Member** are eligible; those marked **Observer** are declined.
- NOB / International Delegates marked as voting members are eligible where applicable.
- A delegate must complete congress check-in before voting-session check-in.
- Duplicate voting check-ins are detected and the original voting check-in time is shown.
- Scanner results now use the current delegate category name and show the stored voting status.
