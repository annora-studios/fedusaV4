# FEDUSA Congress Management Platform v5.5

## Camera capture
- Added Take Photo and Upload Photo controls to every delegate headshot field.
- Supports phone/tablet cameras and desktop/laptop webcams through `getUserMedia`.
- Includes live preview, Capture, Retake, Use Photo and upload fallback.
- Stops camera tracks after capture, close or fallback.
- Resizes images to a maximum dimension of 1200 px and compresses them to JPEG before submission.
- Adds circular preview plus Retake and Remove actions.
- Provides camera permission and unsupported-browser messaging.

## Admin deletion
- Retains the v5.4.2 permanent registration deletion workflow.
- Uses a single clear confirmation dialog; no typed confirmation is required.
- Deletes linked delegates, headshots, QR assets, registration records and index entries.
- Refreshes the registration and audit views after deletion.
