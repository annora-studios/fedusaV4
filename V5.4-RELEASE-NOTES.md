# FEDUSA Congress Management Platform v5.4

## Email notification module

- NOB / International Delegate confirmation emails are sent to the delegate.
- Affiliate confirmation emails are sent only to the logistics contact.
- Confirmation emails include the registration reference, registration summary and secure management link.
- QR codes and badge links are intentionally excluded from emails.
- Registration update emails include a clear change summary.
- Delegate-facing emails support English, French, Portuguese and Spanish.
- Admin notifications remain in English and cover:
  - New NOB / International Delegate registration
  - New affiliate registration
  - Registration updated
  - Registration cancelled
- Added admin cancellation action and audit entry.
- Admin notification recipients are configured with `ADMIN_NOTIFICATION_EMAILS`.
